# Post-fight presentation — 0.70

After the final round's KO presentation, the winner performs her looping victory animation for five seconds while the loser stays in her final floor pose. Combat and projectiles stop. Pause suspends the presentation timer; Enter and Space cannot skip it.

Story and Arcade then show a fight stats popup with both fighters' damage, hits, hit rate, best combo, throws, counters, round score and duration. Enter, Space or the Continue button advances to the existing dialog, continue screen or Arcade score screen. Versus and Local retain their existing result options after the same victory scene.

Round banners now sit above the fighters so intro poses remain visible. Knockdown descent selects falling art, and the existing wake-up interval presents both down and recovery sequences without changing combat timers. The loser no longer idles after a match, and victory animation clocks keep advancing.

Story faceoff, aftermath and ending portraits use square panels with aspect-preserving image fitting. Other framed portraits also preserve their source proportions. Aftermath shows the opponent from the completed fight.

Validation covers animation timing, all four match modes, pause, result input gating, route progression, draw handling, actual rendered frames, unlock behavior and core combat parity.
