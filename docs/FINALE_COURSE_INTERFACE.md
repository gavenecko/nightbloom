# Haunted Finale, course stats and play controls

## Haunted Finale

A super that delivers the match-winning knockout automatically starts a 36-hit barrage. In the default first-to-two format this requires already having one round win. A first-round KO, a nonlethal super, a normal attack, or Training does not trigger it. Custom match formats use their actual deciding win.

The lethal contact counts as hit one. Thirty-five rapid overkill impacts follow over roughly 1.8 seconds; the KO and round victory are awarded once the sequence ends. The defender stays at zero HP, and the overkill hits add no damage. They do count toward hits and highest combo. Both players and CPU opponents can trigger it.

All fighters use their approved painted super effect and shared impact artwork. There are no added full-screen flashes. Reduced motion freezes the effect texture and removes the repeated displacement/shake. Effects remain visible with decorative particles disabled.

## Course summary

Story and Arcade start fresh course counters when a course begins. Completed matches, including losses/retries and secret challenger matches, contribute once each. The reliquary intermission is excluded from match totals.

The completion summary lists wins/matches, losses, total hits, peak combo, damage dealt/taken, attack and super attempts, throw attempts, counters, blocked attacks dealt, Finales, overkill hits, and simulated match time. Paused time is excluded. It appears before the ending presentation, and updates after a secret challenger is defeated. The last summary can be reopened from Controls & settings during the current page session.

## Returning to the menu

Escape opens a confirmation instead of immediately abandoning the match or course. Keep playing is focused by default. Escape cancels the confirmation; Enter activates the focused button. Controller A/B and left/right navigation are supported. While the dialog is open, simulation and inputs are suspended. Earned unlocks remain saved.

Escape still skips the opening sequence. Backspace retains its existing back-navigation behavior.

## Controls & settings

The tools below the play screen are inside one collapsed Controls & settings dropdown. Existing training, voice, effects, inspector and debug controls remain available there.

Touch controls offer Automatic, On, and Off. Automatic uses a coarse pointer or touch-capable device signal. The first mobile layout has an eight-direction joystick, light/heavy attacks, direct Special A/B buttons, super, throw, held guard, jump, pause, Confirm and Menu. It supports simultaneous joystick/button touches and releases input on pointer cancellation, capture loss, focus loss and hidden pages.

This is the initial mobile control layout. Physical-phone sizing, browser audio activation and sustained touch play still need a device playtest; keyboard/controller play remains supported.
