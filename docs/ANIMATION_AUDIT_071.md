# Animation visibility — 0.71

All 18 animation categories are connected for all 12 fighters (216 render selections checked). Some actions are conditional: falling follows descent, down/recover follows knockdowns, tech recovery follows an escape, and intro/victory belongs to the round and match flow. Air and crouching attacks intentionally reuse the light/heavy artwork.

Super activation retains its existing 30-tick combat freeze, meter cost and move timings, with a compact name banner above the fighting area. The full-screen dimming, large portrait, diagonal rails and activation camera zoom were removed so both fighters remain visible. The ordinary announcement yields to the super banner.

Short dashes now use their 10-tick duration to display all four poses. Ground and air tech escapes carry an 18-tick visual recovery interval that cannot override attacks or hit/block stun; collision, movement and invulnerability retain their existing rules. A visible pause indicator explains how to resume, including automatic focus-loss pauses.

Verified with actual asset renders, animation timing, five-second victory/stat flow, unlock persistence, and 100 core combat parity cases.
