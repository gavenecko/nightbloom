# Belladonna — the eleventh starting queen

Belladonna fills the fourteenth character-select slot: eleven starting fighters and three secret fighters. She uses the approved nightshade flowercap, purple petal dress, four lavender wings, body proportions, portrait, and selected voice B.

Her 80 approved source frames cover twenty actions, including separate airborne light and heavy attacks. Intro grows her from tiny fairy size to combat size; victory shrinks her into a small hovering fairy and holds that final size. These are visual presentation changes; combat hurtboxes remain consistent.

Moves: Fairy Dust (special A), Nightshade Gale (special B projectile), Midnight Bloom (100-meter super). All three use approved painted effects. The larger super, volume controls, pause move list, and spoken super name are supported.

Belladonna has her own ten-opponent course, recorded dialogue, congratulations garden background, and second-clear Bonus Ending. Every other route includes an encounter with her while keeping its original final opponent. She is a starting queen, so her course leads to Arachne under the existing challenger rules. Arachne → Living Doll → Narrator remains unchanged; only winning each secret fight unlocks that fighter.

Bonus Group now includes Belladonna in the volleyball scene. It requires earning all fourteen Bonus Endings, including hers. Existing earned endings and secret wins are preserved. A player who already earned the previous thirteen Bonus Endings needs Belladonna's Bonus Ending to view the updated group reward.

Art production sources and prompts are preserved in the workspace's outputs/nightshade-fairy-design, outputs/belladonna-art-review, and outputs/belladonna-painted-effects. The fighter atlas manifest records source hashes and cell bounds; each approved source is retained separately. No approved fighter design or voice has been replaced.
