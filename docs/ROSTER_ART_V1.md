# Roster art pass 0.54

Updated matching portraits for Vampira, Nekomata, Banshee and Medusa. Jiangshi retains her approved portrait. New full-body concept references and square portraits establish designs for Yurei, Dullahan, Werewolf, Mummy and Witch.

The five remaining fighters keep their existing animation sheets until their individual animation passes. Gameplay, collision and all existing body/FX atlases are unchanged.

All game portraits are separate 256x256 PNGs under `assets/portraits/v1/`. Full design sheets are under `assets/designs/v1/`. View the art in `roster-gallery.html`.

Artwork created with the built-in image generator. Exact prompts, references and the Dullahan lance framing correction are recorded in `ROSTER_ART_V1_PROMPTS.json`. Deterministic resizing and padding use Sharp; no generated anatomy was programmatically altered.
